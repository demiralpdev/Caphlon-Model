03 · QUICKSTART & HARDWARE PANEL 03 // DEPLOY

Deploy in Under 60 Seconds

Compatible with standard llama.cpp and Ollama runtimes out of the box.

llama-cli -hf ssuasd/Caphlon-Model:caphlon-27b-iq2_s.gguf -p "Explain quantum superposition in three plain sentences."
  • Memory: 8 GB Unified Memory (Apple Silicon M1/M2/M3/M4) or 8 GB system RAM.
  • Backend: Apple Metal, Vulkan, CUDA, or pure CPU vector extensions.
  • Throughput: ~38.4 tokens/second on M3 Max, ~18.2 tokens/second on base M1 MacBook Air.
