Quickstart

llama-server -m Caphlon-Model-IQ2_S.gguf \
  -ngl 99 -c 32768 -np 1 \
  -b 4096 -ub 1024 --flash-attn on \
  --spec-type ngram-mod --spec-ngram-mod-n-match 24 \
  --spec-ngram-mod-n-min 48 --spec-ngram-mod-n-max 64 \
  --jinja --port 8080

Measured on this build: 29.7 tok/s on file-edit workloads (2.34× the baseline 12.7), 12.4 tok/s on free-form prose. Send chat_template_kwargs: {"enable_thinking": false} or the model will spend your context on internal reasoning.
